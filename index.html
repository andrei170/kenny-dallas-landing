FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need prior sales experience?

No. Most of my students come from non-sales backgrounds. Retail, trades, military, hospitality. The framework is built to teach the skill from zero. What matters more than experience is reps - how willing you are to practice mock calls and take feedback.

How long until I'm making money?

Most students land their first closing contract inside 30-45 days. First commission usually hits inside 60 days. The 60-day placement commitment means if you haven't landed a gig by then, I keep working with you until you do.

What's the time commitment?

Plan on 10-15 hours a week during the training phase. Once you're placed on an offer, the time is whatever you want it to be - most full-time closers work 25-35 hours a week and do $10K-$20K months.

Will you help me find an offer to close for?

Yes. Direct introductions to offers my network is actively hiring closers for is part of the program. I don't guarantee you'll land every offer I introduce you to - you still have to interview - but you won't be job-hunting on your own.

Who is this NOT for?

People looking for passive income, get-rich-quick, or a side hustle they can phone in. Closing is a skill that pays. You have to practice, take feedback, and show up on calls. If you want the income without the reps, this isn't it.
